Macroeconomic Regime History & Asset Normalization

Historical business cycle partitioning via US Industrial Production, CPI, and 10Y-2Y Treasury spread momentum

ⓘ How to Interpret This Dashboard
GOLDILOCKS Accelerating Growth + Decelerating Inflation

Primary Asset: SPY (S&P 500)
Companies grow their profits without high interest rates slowing them down. Stocks typically perform best in this environment.

REFLATION Accelerating Growth + Accelerating Inflation

Primary Asset: DBC (Commodities)
Strong economic demand outpaces the supply of raw materials. Physical commodities (like oil and metals) grow faster than stocks or bonds.

STAGFLATION Decelerating Growth + Accelerating Inflation

Primary Asset: GLD (Gold)
Rising costs shrink company profits while central banks raise rates to fight inflation. Physical gold acts as a safe haven to protect your money.

DEFLATION Decelerating Growth + Decelerating Inflation

Primary Asset: TLT (20Y+ Treasuries)
The economy slows down, forcing central banks to cut interest rates. As rates fall, long-term government bonds jump in value.
