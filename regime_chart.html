ⓘ How to Interpret This Dashboard
GOLDILOCKS Accelerating Growth + Decelerating Inflation

Primary Asset: SPY (S&P 500)
Corporate margins expand without central bank interest rate pressure. Equities historically dominate this regime with low macro drag.

REFLATION Accelerating Growth + Accelerating Inflation

Primary Asset: DBC (Commodities)
Aggregate consumer and industrial demand outstrips raw material capacity. Real physical assets appreciate faster than financial paper assets.

STAGFLATION Decelerating Growth + Accelerating Inflation

Primary Asset: GLD (Gold)
Rising production costs compress company earnings while monetary authorities hike borrowing rates. Physical gold protects purchasing power.

DEFLATION Decelerating Growth + Decelerating Inflation

Primary Asset: TLT (20Y+ Treasuries)
Demand contraction prompts emergency central bank policy rate reductions. Plunging yields drive capital gains in long-duration sovereign paper.
